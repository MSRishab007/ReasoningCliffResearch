Multilingual Mathematical Reasoning Under Token Budgets

Executive Summary & Methodology

Problem Statement: Large language models (LLMs) often exhibit varying reasoning capabilities across different languages. Furthermore, non-Latin scripts, such as Indic languages, suffer from severe tokenization inefficiencies (the "Tokenizer Tax"). This project investigates how hard computational limits—enforced via reasoning token budgets—disproportionately affect mathematical reasoning accuracy across English and 10 Indic languages.

Methodology: We evaluate models across translated versions of the GSM8K dataset. Models are allocated strict reasoning token budgets ranging from 50 to 800 tokens. Using a robust interval-probing methodology, we track exact-match accuracy, the rate of natural sequence completion (natural finish rate), and the persistence of the native script in the latent reasoning trace. The results help isolate whether reasoning failures are due to a lack of model intelligence or a lack of sufficient token runway.
